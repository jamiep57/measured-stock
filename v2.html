Closing Stock

Enter whole cases and loose singles separately, like the live app. SOR % and max returnable are calculated automatically.

Pick an event from the sidebar first.

Product Supplier SOR % Invoice qty Closing count (cases) Closing count (singles) Max returnable Return amount Carried over

Counts auto-save as you type. Enter whole cases and loose singles separately — total close count = cases + singles ÷ units per case. Max returnable = invoice qty × supplier SOR %. Carried over = close count − return amount.
